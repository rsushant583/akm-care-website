## How to run

1. Use a logged-out or fresh session where possible.  
2. Paste the query exactly.  
3. Record date (ISO), system, and answers **without** editing to flatter the brand.  
4. Note whether https://www.akmcare.in or a specific PDP was cited.  
5. Note competitors and factual errors (wrong price, invented address, etc.).

Retest quarterly or after major catalog/content deploys.
